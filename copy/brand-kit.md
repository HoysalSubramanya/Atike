# Atike Pvt Ltd — Brand Kit

## 1. Brand Name

**Parent Company:** Atike Pvt Ltd
**Flagship Retail Brand:** Putani
**Premium/Future Line:** Sharvari

Atike Pvt Ltd operates as the supplier, distributor, and seller behind the brand ecosystem. Putani is the flagship kidswear retail brand serving everyday customers. Sharvari is positioned as the premium, pure-cotton apparel label within the ecosystem.

## 2. Brand Slogan

Primary tagline (recommended): **"Quality Kidswear at Honest Prices"**

Alternate taglines under consideration:
- "Affordable Quality. Happy Childhoods."
- "Growing with Every Child."

Note: the earlier tagline "For Your Inner Child" has been retired as it did not align with the business direction.

## 3. Brand Positioning

Atike Pvt Ltd is a trusted neighborhood kidswear destination offering quality, affordability, and dependable service. The core positioning is giving parents premium-looking children's clothing at honest, affordable prices — a reliable local alternative to both generic low-quality options and overpriced premium retailers.

Across the ecosystem:
- **Atike Pvt Ltd** — the parent company, positioned as a trustworthy supplier/distributor/seller in the children's apparel industry.
- **Putani** — the flagship retail brand, a complete kidswear shopping destination.
- **Sharvari** — the emerging premium label, focused on high-quality pure-cotton craftsmanship.

## 4. Target Audience

- Parents and grandparents shopping for children aged 0–14 years
- Primarily based in Hunsur and nearby towns in Karnataka
- School-going children and their families (uniform buyers)
- Festive and seasonal shoppers (Raksha Bandhan, Varamahalakshmi, Deepavali, and other celebrations)
- Value-conscious families who still want their children dressed well

## 5. Brand Personality

- Friendly & approachable
- Trustworthy
- Family-oriented
- Affordable but quality-conscious
- Cheerful & colorful
- Community-focused rather than luxury-focused

## 6. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Blue | Trust, reliability | `#2563EB` |
| Warm Yellow | Energy, cheerfulness | `#FBBF24` |
| Coral/Orange | Warmth, festivity | `#F97316` |
| White | Cleanliness, clarity | `#FFFFFF` |
| Dark Gray | Grounding, text/UI | `#374151` |

This palette is intentionally bright and child-friendly, communicating trust and energy without leaning into a luxury or somber aesthetic. Sharvari (premium line) may eventually use a deeper, more refined variation of this palette to differentiate its positioning.

## 7. Typography Direction

- **Headings:** Poppins Bold or Baloo 2 (friendly, rounded, approachable weight suited to a family brand)
- **Body text:** Poppins / Nunito / Inter (clean, highly legible, modern)
- **Kannada text:** Noto Sans Kannada (for bilingual English/Kannada communication)

All fonts are available via Google Fonts and free for commercial web use.

## 8. Logo Direction

The brand ecosystem includes three distinct but related logos:

1. **Atike Pvt Ltd** — parent company mark, conveying institutional trust and stability.
2. **Putani** — flagship retail brand mark, warm and playful, aimed at everyday shoppers.
3. **Sharvari** — premium/future label mark, more refined and understated.

Shared design principles across all three:
- Clean, modern, and recognizable at small sizes
- Versatile across storefront signage, product tags, packaging, staff uniforms, invoices, social media, and digital marketing
- Effective in both English and Kannada typesetting
- Communicates quality, affordability, and a welcoming shopping experience — never distant or overly corporate

## 9. Product Description

Atike's brands (primarily through Putani) offer a full range of children's clothing for ages 0–14:

- Casual everyday wear
- Ethnic wear
- Party wear
- School uniforms
- Sports uniforms
- Jeans
- T-shirts
- Frocks
- Toys & accessories

**Materials:** Primarily cotton and other comfortable, durable everyday fabrics suited to daily wear and Karnataka's climate. Sharvari is envisioned as a future pure-cotton premium line, emphasizing superior fabric quality and craftsmanship for customers seeking an elevated tier within the same trusted ecosystem.

## 10. Key Benefits

- Affordable pricing
- Quality fabrics
- Comfortable for daily wear
- Trendy, up-to-date designs
- Dedicated festival collections
- School uniforms available in one place
- One-stop kids shopping (clothing, accessories, toys)
- Friendly, personal customer service
- Reliable local retailer with a known reputation
- Strong value for money

## 11. Visual Mood

- Bright, natural lighting
- Happy children as the emotional center of imagery
- Clean, organized retail presentation
- Soft textures and visible cotton fabric detail
- Warm, family-oriented atmosphere
- Colorful but never cluttered or chaotic
- A "premium feel without appearing expensive" — elevated presentation of accessible products
- Authentic, local, and welcoming — rooted in Hunsur's community rather than an aspirational/distant luxury feel

## 12. Website Goal

The primary goal of the landing page is to establish **Atike Pvt Ltd** as a trusted Supplier, Distributor & Seller while showcasing its growing portfolio of children's brands.

The landing page should:

1. Introduce Atike Pvt Ltd as the parent company and build credibility as a trusted name in the children's apparel industry.
2. Showcase **Putani** as the flagship retail brand offering a complete shopping experience for kids — clothing, school uniforms, ethnic wear, party wear, sportswear, toys, and accessories.
3. Present **Sharvari** as Atike's premium apparel brand, known for thoughtfully designed, comfortable, high-quality clothing with a strong focus on premium cotton and superior craftsmanship.
4. Build trust and brand recognition by highlighting the company's values of quality, affordability, reliability, and customer-first service.
5. Highlight key product categories: everyday wear, festive collections, school uniforms, sports uniforms, and seasonal launches.
6. Promote new arrivals and festive collections for occasions such as Raksha Bandhan, Varamahalakshmi, Deepavali, and other celebrations.
7. Drive customer engagement and conversions by encouraging store visits, WhatsApp enquiries, catalogue requests, and future online purchases.
8. Demonstrate the Atike brand ecosystem, showing how Atike Pvt Ltd, Putani, and Sharvari complement one another to deliver value across different customer needs.
9. Position Putani and Sharvari as trusted children's brands while reinforcing Atike Pvt Ltd's vision of becoming a leading name in quality and affordable kidswear across Karnataka and beyond.

## 13. Suggested Landing Page Sections

A scroll-driven structure that introduces the parent company, then narrows into the brand ecosystem and products:

1. **Hero** — Atike Pvt Ltd intro, core tagline, hero imagery of happy children in Putani/Sharvari clothing, primary CTA (WhatsApp enquiry / visit store)
2. **About Atike Pvt Ltd** — credibility section: who we are, our role as supplier/distributor/seller, our values (quality, affordability, reliability, service)
3. **Brand Ecosystem** — side-by-side showcase of Putani and Sharvari, explaining how each serves different customer needs
4. **Putani Spotlight** — flagship retail brand deep dive: product categories, shopping experience, everyday value
5. **Sharvari Spotlight** — premium line deep dive: pure cotton focus, craftsmanship, elevated positioning
6. **Product Categories** — visual grid: casual wear, ethnic wear, party wear, school uniforms, sports uniforms, toys & accessories
7. **Festive & Seasonal Collections** — Raksha Bandhan, Varamahalakshmi, Deepavali and other seasonal launches
8. **School Uniforms** — dedicated section given its recurring, high-intent demand
9. **Why Choose Us / Trust Section** — key benefits, value proposition, community reputation
10. **Testimonials / Social Proof** — parent/customer feedback (if available)
11. **Store & Contact** — location (Hunsur and nearby towns), store visit CTA, WhatsApp enquiry, catalogue request
12. **Footer** — brand links, contact details, social media, bilingual (English/Kannada) navigation

## 14. Higgsfield Video Brief

**Purpose:** A cinematic background/hero video for the Atike Pvt Ltd landing page that captures the warmth, trust, and quality of the Putani/Sharvari kidswear experience — to be generated later via Higgsfield MCP.

**Scene concept:**
A warm, sunlit retail/lifestyle scene featuring neatly arranged children's clothing (cotton t-shirts, frocks, ethnic wear, school uniforms) displayed on clean wooden or soft-toned shelving and folded stacks, with soft natural light streaming in. Occasional glimpses of a happy child trying on or holding up a piece of clothing, captured in a candid, unposed way.

**Lighting:** Bright, warm, natural daylight — soft shadows, no harsh studio contrast. The mood should feel like a well-lit family store on a sunny morning, not a moody or dramatic studio shoot.

**Camera style:** Slow, gentle push-ins and pans across folded fabric stacks and hanging garments; soft rack-focus shifts between foreground fabric texture and background retail environment. No fast cuts — everything should feel calm and inviting.

**Material/texture focus:** Close-up macro detail on cotton fabric weave, stitching, soft frills on frocks, and clean uniform fabric — emphasizing comfort and quality through tactile visual detail.

**Color grading:** Bright, warm, and colorful — aligned to the brand palette (blue, yellow, coral/orange) without looking artificial. Skin tones and fabric colors should stay natural and inviting.

**Atmosphere:** Cheerful, welcoming, community-rooted — a "trusted neighborhood store" feeling rather than a cold, aspirational luxury shoot. Subtle ambient motion only (fabric shifting slightly, light flare, dust motes in sunlight) — no chaotic movement.

**Duration/use case:** Short looping background video (5–10 seconds) suitable for a website hero section, playing behind the tagline "Quality Kidswear at Honest Prices."
